# StoreTrack — shopping lists that follow you through the store

Ever get home and realize you forgot the one thing you actually went out for?
Or find yourself doubling back across the store because "eggs" was three items
below "bananas" on your list?

**StoreTrack fixes that.** It keeps a separate list for each store you shop at
— and here's the good part: you tell it how *your* store is laid out. Produce
first, then bakery, then aisle 7. Your list automatically sorts itself into
those sections, in the order you actually walk. No more zigzagging. Just work
your way down the list and out the door.

**What makes it nice to use:**

🛒 **A list per store.** Costco stuff stays on the Costco list. No mental
filtering.

🚶 **Sorted your way.** Group items into sections like "Produce" or "Aisle 2,"
and drag them into your walking order. Shopping becomes a straight line.

✅ **Tap to check off.** Items you grab slide down into a "Checked" group, so
what's left is always right at the top. Done with the trip? One tap unchecks
everything for next time.

👨‍👩‍👧 **Share with your household.** Everyone in your family sees the same lists,
updating live. Add milk from the couch, and whoever's already at the store sees
it appear.

⌨️ **It remembers what you buy.** Start typing "milk" and it suggests the things
you've bought before — no retyping "Silk Almond Milk" every week.

👀 **See who did what.** A running activity feed shows every add and check-off,
labeled with who made it.

It's free, there's no app store to deal with, and it works on your phone and
your computer.

## Try it

**👉 [storetrack-xi.vercel.app](https://storetrack-xi.vercel.app)**

Tap **Continue with Google** — that's the whole sign-up, no new password to
remember. Then either start a new household or join someone else's with their
6-character invite code (they'll find it on their Settings screen).

## Put it on your home screen

It only takes a few seconds, and afterward it opens like any other app — no
browser bar, its own icon.

**On Android:** Open the link in Chrome, tap the **⋮** menu in the corner, and
choose **Add to Home screen** → **Install**.

**On iPhone:** Open the link in **Safari** (this part only works in Safari), tap
the **Share** button at the bottom, scroll down, and choose **Add to Home
Screen**.

That's it. Happy shopping! 🎉
